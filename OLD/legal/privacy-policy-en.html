Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the Royal Multi Gamers Launcher ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application.

2. Information Collection

The launcher only collects technical information necessary for its operation:

  • Game server status (availability, player count)
  • Game server connection information (IP address, port)
  • Temporary configuration data stored locally

3. Use of Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Display real-time game server status
  • Facilitate connection to game servers
  • Display relevant community news

4. Data Storage

The launcher does not store any personal data. All information is temporary and stored locally on your device during the session.

5. Third-Party Services

The launcher interacts with the following services:

  • Game servers (CSS, CS:GO, ECO, BattleBit)
  • Discord (for community links)
  • Royal Multi Gamers website

The use of these third-party services is subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of information.

7. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your information
  • Rectify or delete your information
  • Object to the processing of your data
  • File a complaint with a supervisory authority

8. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us via:

  • Our Discord server: https://discord.gg/tqPtgyk6yT
  • Our website: https://clan-rmg.com
